Privacy
The short version, in plain words.
What we keep
  • Your name and email
  • Your profile picture, if you sign in with Google or Facebook

Who sees it

Only you. Each ledger is private. Nothing is sold or shared.

Sign-in providers

Google and Facebook only confirm who you are. They never see your ledger.
